Welcome email

Tells players where the app is, how to sign in with a code, and how to put it on their home screen (iPhone and Android). It opens a ready-to-send draft in Gmail (or your computer's email app) with everyone in BCC, so it comes from you and you hit Send. Long lists can be too much for some email apps; if nothing opens or addresses are missing, use the copy buttons instead.

Schedule

"Rain out" moves that week and every later week back by 7 days, so the season ends a week later.

Tee sheet

Tee times stay put. Drag a matchup by its handle (or use the arrows) to move it to another tee time.

Tee times and season build

The league's tee times are the same every week: the first time, then one every few minutes. Changing them here updates every week that isn't final.

"Build the season" fills every week that isn't final. Each team plays every other team once in the first half and again in the second half, so a rematch is half a season away. Teams rotate through the tee times so no team always goes first or last. You can still drag matchups in any week afterwards.

Courses

Rating, slope and par per tee for each nine. These drive handicaps.

Lineups & results

Each team plays its two regulars unless you change it here. Pick a sub from the list, or Ghost (locked at the season's ghost net score). Scores can be fixed in the grid; finalizing saves the points and adds each round to the player's handicap.

Scores

Every golfer in the lineups, hole by hole. Type to fix a score; it saves when you leave the box.

Queen City Cup

Match play between two sides, run by Article XII of the rulebook. Captains pick formats and enter lineups in the app; a lineup stays hidden until the other captain responds or you reveal the round.

Cup history
