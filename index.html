League rules

BCC Golf League Rules

2027 changes
  • Ghost scores are locked at 36 net (replaces the weekly league average in Article VII).
  • No singles matches: the 5-point singles bonus (Article IV) and singles putt concessions (Article VIII) are gone.

Tap an article to open it.

I – Purpose and Spirit of the League

The purpose of the BCC Golf League is to provide a competitive, fair, and enjoyable golf experience for players of all skill levels.

While league standings, championships, and weekly competition are important, the league exists primarily to promote fellowship, sportsmanship, and enjoyment of the game.

All players are expected to:

  • Conduct themselves with honesty and integrity.
  • Respect fellow competitors, course staff, and league officials.
  • Apply league rules fairly and consistently.
  • Resolve disputes in a respectful manner.
  • Act in the spirit of the game even when a specific situation is not addressed within the rulebook.

The Board and Commissioner reserve the right to interpret rules and make decisions consistent with the spirit and intent of the league.

II – League Organization

League Structure

  • The league shall consist of ten (10) teams.
  • Each team shall consist of two (2) rostered players.
  • The regular season shall consist of eighteen (18) scheduled weeks.
  • Team and Individual Champions shall be determined by regular season results.

League Governance

The league shall be governed by a Board consisting of four (4) members, including the Commissioner.

Board Responsibilities

The Board shall be responsible for:

  • Maintaining league rules.
  • Approving rule changes.
  • Resolving disputes.
  • Reviewing handicap concerns.
  • Addressing situations not specifically covered within this Rulebook.
  • Administering weekly betting games.

Quorum

A quorum shall consist of three (3) Board members.

Rule Changes

League rule changes shall require approval of at least three (3) Board members.

Unless otherwise specified, rule changes shall take effect at the start of the following season.

Commissioner Authority

The Commissioner shall administer league operations, scheduling, scoring, handicaps, standings, and rule interpretation.

The Commissioner may issue temporary rulings during the season when immediate decisions are required. Such rulings may be reviewed by the Board upon request.

III – Handicap System and Tee Selection

Tee Selection

The league utilizes World Handicap System (WHS) principles to allow equitable competition from multiple tee boxes.

Available tee boxes:

  • Blue Tees (Default)
  • White Tees (Optional for any player)
  • Gold Tees (Available to players age 65 and older and required for 14 and younger)

Players shall declare their tee selection prior to Week 1.

Unless otherwise declared, players shall compete from the Blue Tees.

Handicap Index Calculation

Definitions:

  • Tee Difficulty = Slope Rating ÷ 113
  • Scratch Par = Course Rating

Variation for each round:

Variation = (Gross Score – Scratch Par) ÷ Tee Difficulty

Handicap Index:

Average of the lowest eight (8) Variations from the player’s most recent twenty (20) rounds.

Daily Handicap

Definitions:

  • Tee Adjustment = Scratch Par – Par

Formula:

Handicap = (Handicap Index × Tee Difficulty) + Tee Adjustment

The league scoring software shall maintain official handicaps.

IV – Weekly Competition

Individual Points

Players earn weekly points as follows:

  • 1st Place = 20 points
  • 2nd Place = 19 points
  • Continuing through:
  • 20th Place = 1 point

Singles Match Bonus

A player shall earn five (5) additional points for defeating their designated weekly singles opponent.

Weekly Point Total

Weekly Point Total = Placement Points + Singles Match Bonus

V – Individual Championship

Absences

Absent players shall receive zero (0) individual points for the week.

Substitute and ghost scores shall not generate individual points for absent players.

Dropped Scores

At the conclusion of the season, each player’s three (3) lowest weekly point totals shall be discarded.

All weekly point totals, including absence weeks, are eligible for removal.

Final standings shall be determined using each player’s best fifteen (15) weekly point totals.

Individual Championship Tiebreakers

  1. Head-to-head result.
  2. Gross Ringer Match Play.

Gross Ringer Match Play shall compare each player’s lowest gross score recorded on each hole throughout the season.

The player winning the greater number of holes shall receive the higher standing.

VI – Team Championship

Weekly Team Match

Each weekly team matchup shall award:

  • Win = 10 Team Points
  • Tie = 5 Team Points
  • Loss = 0 Team Points

Team Championship

The Team Championship shall be determined by total Team Points accumulated during the regular season.

Team Championship Tiebreakers

  1. Head-to-head result.
  2. Combined Individual Championship point totals of rostered players.
VII – Substitute Players and Ghost Scores

Substitute Players

Substitute players may be used when a rostered player is unavailable.

Substitute scores shall count toward team competition only.

Substitute scores shall not generate individual points for absent players.

Ghost Scores

When no substitute is available, a ghost score shall be used. There are two kinds:

Ghost – Average: the absent player’s average net score over their last three (3) rounds on the nine being played, including rounds from previous seasons, rounded to the nearest whole number.

Ghost – Set Score: 36 net, used when the absent player has fewer than three (3) rounds on that nine.

The ghost score is shown on the tee sheet before play.

Sub Score

The Sub Score is the substitute’s actual net score. It counts for the team from the substitute’s third (3rd) round on a nine. For the substitute’s first two (2) rounds on that nine, the team counts the absent player’s ghost score (Ghost – Average or Ghost – Set Score) instead.

Ghost scores shall count only for team competition.

VIII – Local Rules

Lost Ball / Out of Bounds

Any ball that is lost or out of bounds may be dropped laterally at the point where the ball was lost or crossed out of bounds with a one-stroke penalty.

This local rule replaces stroke-and-distance relief.

Fairway Relief

A ball lying in a divot, repaired divot, tire track, sprinkler repair scar, or similar abnormal fairway damage may be lifted and placed within one club length:

  • No closer to the hole.
  • Must remain in the fairway.
  • No penalty.

Maximum Score Per Hole

The maximum score on any hole shall be double par.

Par Maximum Score
3 6
4 8
5 10

Once a player reaches double par, the player shall pick up and record the maximum score.

Conceded Putts

A player’s designated weekly singles opponent may concede a putt at any time.

A conceded putt shall be considered holed with the next stroke.

Only the designated singles opponent may grant a concession.

IX – Pace of Play

The league encourages Ready Golf and efficient play at all times.

Players should:

  • Play when safe and ready.
  • Limit ball searches whenever practical.
  • Be prepared when it is their turn.
  • Record scores promptly.
  • Proceed to the next tee without unnecessary delay.
  • Pick up once double par has been reached.

The goal of the league is to complete each round in a reasonable amount of time while preserving competitive integrity.

X – Weather and Schedule Adjustments

If league play is canceled due to weather, course closure, or other circumstances beyond league control, the canceled week shall be moved to the end of the regular season schedule.

The Commissioner may make reasonable scheduling adjustments when necessary.

XI – Final Authority

Situations not specifically addressed within this Constitution shall be resolved by the Commissioner and Board in a manner consistent with the spirit, intent, and fairness of league competition.
